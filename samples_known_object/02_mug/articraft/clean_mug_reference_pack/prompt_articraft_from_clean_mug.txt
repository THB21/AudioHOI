Create a simple 3D mug proxy from the clean mug reference image.

This asset is for contact reasoning, not photorealistic reconstruction.

Required semantic parts:
- body
- handle
- rim
- bottom

Geometry requirements:
- The body is a vertical cylindrical cup body matching the clean reference proportions.
- The rim is a thin rounded ring.
- The bottom is a flat circular base.
- The handle is a separate semantic part named "handle".
- The handle should be a smooth C-shaped side tube whose two endpoints enter/attach directly into the cup body.
- Do not add external connector pads, wedge blocks, rectangular bars, ribbed joints, or mechanical-looking interfaces.
- The handle must be consistent with the clean reference: when viewed from the side it should project like a narrow vertical curve/strip, not a large front-facing loop.

Keep the geometry simple and robust. The downstream pipeline will convert this asset into proxy/mug_proxy.json and use the handle as the object-side contact region.
