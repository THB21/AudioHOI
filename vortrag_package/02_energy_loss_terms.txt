ENERGY / LOSS TERMS  —  E_total = sum of weighted per-frame residuals
(no learned prior, no z=f*R/r sphere cue; all geometric/observational)

  E_2d       center reprojection   | projected 3D center vs 2D obs        | px
  E_contact  contact gap           | object <-> contact body part        | m
  E_depth    metric depth          | |solved tz - DA3 depth|             | m
  E_support  floor                 | object bottom vs floor line         | px
  E_smooth   smoothness (R_reg)    | translation acceleration (jerk)     | m/frame^2
